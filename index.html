What it does

The PC look, on a Quest.

Everything runs on the headset itself. No PC, no streaming, no file manager.

ReShade presets

Colour grading, LUTs, grain, vignette, CRT and glitch. Bodycam, Night Vision, Cinematic, Noir and more out of the box.

Bring your own .ini

Drop a ReShade preset in UserData/QuestShade/ReShade/. Colour shaders match exactly; anything a Quest can't run is skipped and listed.

Grab-and-film camera

Squeeze near a handle and it snaps into your hand. Tap the trigger for a photo, hold it to record. POV, chest, selfie, tripod and more.

Bloom + depth of field

Focus follows what you point at, and bloom calibrates itself to every map so bright levels don't blow out.

Straight to your gallery

Videos with game audio and mic, encoded like the Quest's own captures, saved right where the Quest keeps them.

Timeline replays

Got Timeline? Film any replay person from their POV, chest or over the shoulder, with Timeline's controls inside the QuestShade menu.

Install

Four steps, about a minute.

Works on standalone Quest and on PC.

  1. Have MelonLoader on BONELAB and install BoneLib, which gives you the menu and the grabbable camera.
  2. Download the zip and take Mods/QuestShade.dll out of it.
  3. Quest: copy it to /sdcard/MelonLoader/com.StressLevelZero.BONELAB/Mods/ with SideQuest or any file manager.
    PC: drop it in BONELAB/Mods/.
  4. Launch the game and open BoneMenu → QuestShade. Click both thumbsticks to pull out the camera.

Settings live in UserData/QuestShade/ and update live while you play. Only download QuestShade from this page, GitHub or Thunderstore.
