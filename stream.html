Livestream setup

Go live from your Quest in one minute

Paste your QuestShade.ini, pick where you stream, add your key. You get a fresh QuestShade.ini back with every one of your settings kept, ready to go live to YouTube, Twitch, Kick or TikTok.

Everything happens in your browser. Your settings and stream key are never uploaded or saved anywhere.
1

Paste your QuestShade.ini

It's on your Quest in MelonLoader/com.StressLevelZero.BONELAB/UserData/QuestShade/. Paste it, open the file, or drop it here. No file yet? Leave it empty to make a new one.

Empty: a new QuestShade.ini with just the stream settings will be made.
2

Where are you streaming?

Kick and TikTok also show you a server URL. You can paste their full URL in step 3.

3

Paste your stream key

Just the key works, or the full URL with the key on the end.

Your new QuestShade.ini

Fill in your key to get the file.

Preview the [Stream] section (key hidden)

  


  

    
Put it back on your Quest

    
Replace the old file with the new one, keeping the name QuestShade.ini. Use SideQuest's file manager, or the Quest's Files app if you downloaded it on the headset:

    
/sdcard/MelonLoader/com.StressLevelZero.BONELAB/UserData/QuestShade/QuestShade.ini

    
Then in game: BoneMenu > QuestShade > Camera > Livestream > Go live / end stream. If BONELAB is already running, QuestShade picks up the new file within a couple of seconds.
